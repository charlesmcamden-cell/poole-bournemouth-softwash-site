## Logo

`assets/logo.png` / `assets/logo@2x.png` (used in the header and footer) and
`assets/favicon-*.png` / `assets/apple-touch-icon.png` were generated with
Google Gemini directly by the site owner (18 Aug 2026) from a text prompt
describing a shield/house/water-droplet emblem in the site's brand colours,
with no third-party photography or trademarked material involved. The
transparent background was reconstructed in post-processing (the model's
"transparent" export came back as a flattened checkerboard pattern on a
JPEG, which can't hold real alpha — a saturation-threshold pass converted the
checkerboard back to genuine transparency before cropping and resizing).
`assets/favicon.svg` (the original simple house/wave icon) is kept as a
lightweight SVG fallback for browsers that prefer a vector favicon.
